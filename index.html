How the model works

Transparent calculations, assumptions, and limitations.

AWelch's two-sample t-test

Compares approximate mean listening hours across two independent groups. Uses Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom and a two-sided p-value.

BCategory distribution test

Compares the five listening-time frequencies using Pearson's chi-square statistic. When expected counts are small, calculates a fixed-margin Monte Carlo p-value.

CMidpoint approximation

Listening ranges are converted to 0.25, 0.75, 1.50, 2.50, and 3.50 hours. The “more than 3 hours” midpoint is an arbitrary proxy.

DInterpret with care

These are sample comparisons, not proof of a population effect. Small or concentrated new samples may violate normal approximations. The original workbook describes data as synthetic.
